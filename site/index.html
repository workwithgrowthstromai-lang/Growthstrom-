Questions

Straight answers.

Still unsure? Ask us on the audit call. It's free and there's no pitch deck.

We tried an agency before and got nothing. Why is this different?

Because you can check us. Every week you see which ads made sales and what each sale cost. If the numbers don't move, you leave. No contract keeps you here.

Will AI ads look cheap or fake?

No. AI makes the drafts fast, so we can test far more ideas. Our team picks, edits and approves every ad before it runs, in your brand's look and voice.

How fast will I see results?

New ads start running in the first couple of weeks. Most accounts find their first clear winners within the first month. We'll tell you what to expect for your account on the audit call, honestly.

Do I keep my ad accounts and data?

Yes. Your accounts, your pixel, your customer data. We get access, you keep ownership, and you can see everything we do.

Which platforms do you run?

Facebook and Instagram, Google and YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn for software brands. We pick the mix that fits where your buyers are.

What ad budget do I need?

We'll tell you on the audit call. If your budget is too small for us to help, we'll say so and point you to what to do first.
